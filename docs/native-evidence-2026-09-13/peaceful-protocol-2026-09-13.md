## Interpretation

Peaceful mode changes hostile spawning, hunger and regeneration. It tests low
pressure learning and task behavior, not hostile survival or hunger management.
Normal-mode R1/R2 results (60 deaths, no causal multistage success) remain separate.
The 100 ms supported / 50 ms exploratory native planning budgets are unchanged.
Original raw windows and exact actor hashes, not test pass counts, determine
capability claims. More complex scenarios follow demonstrated simpler behavior.
